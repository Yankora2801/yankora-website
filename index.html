Procurement & supply

Better questions lead to clearer comparisons.

When comparing business-to-business offers, the headline price is only one part of the decision. Scope, specifications, delivery terms and after-sales support can matter just as much.

01 / Define

Understand the requirement

Clarify use case, capacity, quantity, space and operating constraints.

02 / Specify

Make offers comparable

Document dimensions, materials, utilities, standards and inclusions.

03 / Compare

Look beyond unit price

Review taxes, freight, warranty, payment terms and lead time.

04 / Confirm

Align supply expectations

Confirm quantities, delivery schedule, order terms and responsibilities.
