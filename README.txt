YANKORA — HOSPITALITY & SUPPLY
================================

Website files
- index.html: complete responsive website (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file)

Before publishing
1. Confirm YANKORA is available for your intended use; check Indian trademarks, company names and domains.
2. Replace the founder photo placeholder with a real photo or remove it.
3. Add a verified founder bio and a real contact route.
4. The category cards are illustrative only. Do not imply inventory, supplier relationships, client relationships, certifications or fulfilment capabilities unless verified.
5. The insight cards are marked as planned guides; publish researched content before presenting them as completed articles.
6. Images and web fonts load from third-party services and require an internet connection.
7. The contact form downloads a local text draft. It does not send or store submissions. Connect a form service or verified email before launch if you want to receive enquiries.

PUBLISH FREE WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Sign in at https://github.com/ and create a new PUBLIC repository, e.g. yankora-hospitality.
2. Upload index.html to the repository root.
3. Open Settings → Pages.
4. Under Build and deployment, select Deploy from a branch.
5. Select branch main and folder /(root), then Save.
6. Wait for publishing. The URL will be https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/yankora-hospitality/

To update the website, edit index.html and commit your changes.


CONTACT FORM
The contact section embeds the Google Form supplied by the site owner. Before launch, test the form on the live site and confirm response collection and notification settings in Google Forms. If embedding is blocked or the form requires sign-in, review the Google Form responder settings and use the direct form link as a fallback.
